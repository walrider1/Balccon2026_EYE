HIBERNATION H // SEALED MANIFEST

203 pods remain sealed with active life support. Montenegrin maintenance crews served a mixed civilian population from across Yugoslavia. Most passengers slept through the crisis; their last ordinary memory may still be a departure announcement.

During the breakdown, disputed identities appeared on return-to-sleep requests. A copy could hide in a capsule as easily as a person could seek refuge. The surviving registers cannot establish which requests completed. Central imposed the strictest lockdown before an independent inspection could take place.

LIVE SUPPORT IS NOT VERIFIED IDENTITY. Nor is unverified identity proof of a copy. There may be hundreds of innocent people behind these doors. Returning the ship gives them a chance and carries an unresolved risk to Earth. Destroying it settles neither question; it merely ensures nobody can answer.
