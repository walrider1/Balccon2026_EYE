ENGINEERING B // REPAIR AUTHORITY

Our mostly Serbian maintenance crews initially shared tools, food and fault reports. Isolation turned a repair schedule into a border. One group controlled oxygen servicing; another held replacement power cells. Access to a valve became more useful than rank.

A technician was accused after refusing to surrender a key. His accusers later admitted that nobody had examined him. A second group used that admission to seize the workshop. We still could not count the copies, but we could count the doors welded shut by people.

The recovery interlock was built to keep one frightened commander from owning every decision. Its independent shares survived our arguments. Read interlock_status.txt for the surviving system record.
