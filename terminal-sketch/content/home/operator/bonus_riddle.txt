PETAR // PHOTOGRAPHS

I hid the photographs when the searches began. A dot before the directory name was enough to keep them off an ordinary listing. I still remember typing ls -a with someone standing behind my chair.

The forensic clerk split the archive seal across three desks. Communications had the first piece. He called his little verification service ctf; the submission form expected flag EYE{...}. I thought it was excessive for a few pictures of us before all this.

I would like someone to see them again.
