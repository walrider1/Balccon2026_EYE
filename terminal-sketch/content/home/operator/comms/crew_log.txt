COMMUNICATIONS // CREW JOURNAL

The captain had been told to bring home a discovery worth the cost of this mission. He kept the Martian sample. After that, every delay became another reason not to admit he might have made a mistake.

We were still heading for Earth. He promised help, asked for patience, and did nothing that we could see. The arguments spread between sectors. Someone asked whether the man giving orders was even the same captain. Nobody had a test that could settle it.

By the last watch the officers were preparing to take control themselves. They had left the recovery procedures and navigation tools ready. The next message from Command never arrived.
