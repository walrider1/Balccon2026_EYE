COMMUNICATIONS F // THREE INTERRUPTED WATCHES

BEFORE THE LOCKS
We were going home from Mars. Yugoslavia had paid for a civilian scientific and settlement mission, and every ministry wanted the discovery that would justify it. The captain sounded smaller after each call from Earth. A strange organism from enclosed underground water became our historic achievement before anyone established whether transporting it was safe.

AFTER THE FIRST DUPLICATES
Reports suggested roughly fifteen possible duplicates across several sectors, but the same name could occur in several messages. A body was found while its owner was reportedly on shift. Someone missing returned with the right memories. Some accusations were retracted; others ended with no surviving witness. Sloki kept asking people to stop turning uncertainty into executions.

AFTER THE LOCKS
The captain isolated the sectors and made himself their only route to one another. Our Macedonian radio watch heard promises of rescue, accusations, contradictory orders and pleas that arrived too late. We could not ask the next sector what it had actually seen. The ship still headed toward Earth while Command deferred every difficult decision. Officers prepared to take control. Someone asked whether the captain himself had been copied. Nobody had evidence. By then a question could get a person killed.

Compare the rescue announcement with evidence.txt. A confident voice is not a transmission receipt.

SECTOR DIRECTORY // ORIGINAL FUNCTIONS
A: Command and navigation, Croatian watch; flight decisions and mission authority.
B: Engineering, Serbian maintenance crews; power, repairs and life-support machinery.
C: Botany, Slovak and Hungarian hydroponics crews; food cultivation and extreme-biology research.
D: Medical, Slovenian clinical teams; treatment, screening and regenerative hibernation.
E: Food and stores, Bosnian and Bosniak crews; inventory and distribution.
F: Communications, Macedonian radio watch; uplinks, local routing and message records.
H: Hibernation, Montenegrin maintenance with mixed civilian passengers; long-duration sleeping berths.
These were overlapping working communities, not national borders. The locks made them borders. Later sector records describe what isolation did to each.
