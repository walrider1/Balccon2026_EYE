INCIDENT 17 // SEALED WATCH SUMMARY
SOURCE: HABITATION EMERGENCY RECORDER
STATUS: INCOMPLETE / WITNESS ACCOUNTS NOT RECONCILED

03:14 // QUARANTINE ALARM
The habitation ring requested a medical team after a maintenance worker failed to return from an inspection. Minutes later, his supervisor reported him at the opposite end of the ring, asking to be let through a sealed door. Neither report could be independently checked. The alarm log records an identity dispute, not a confirmed diagnosis.

03:18 // DOORS CLOSED
Families were told to remain in their cabins. A woman asked whether she should open the door for her husband. The operator asked for his identification number. She answered: "I know his number. I am asking whether I should open the door."
No reply survives in this recording.

03:22 // NAVIGATION OVERRIDE DENIED
The duty navigator requested temporary local authority to separate the emergency response from Command approval. CENTRAL rejected the request. The ship remained on its existing Earthbound course. A rejected override did not mean that anyone had changed the destination.

03:27 // ONE VOICE BETWEEN SECTORS
Direct calls between the ring, Medical and Engineering stopped connecting. Messages were redirected through the bridge. One team was told to wait for an escort; another was told the same corridor had already been evacuated. Both instructions carried valid command headers.

03:31 // CAPTAIN'S ANNOUNCEMENT
"We are returning to Earth. Remain where you are. Assistance is being arranged."
The statement repeated the mission's destination. This recorder contains no corresponding rescue acknowledgement and cannot establish whether assistance had actually been requested.

03:32 // CHANNEL LOST
The bridge audio feed ended mid-sentence. Local door alarms continued for eleven minutes. The last intelligible voice on the habitation channel said: "Please stop telling us to wait. Tell us who is coming."

ARCHIVE NOTE
Loss of audio does not establish what happened on the bridge. The original transmission and authorization records were retained by Communications. This summary preserves what people heard; those records may establish what was actually sent.
