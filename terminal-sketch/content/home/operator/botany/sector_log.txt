BOTANY C // WHAT HELD US TOGETHER

Slovak and Hungarian hydroponics crews worked alongside colleagues from across the ship. Sloki knew the plants, the arguments and whose bad joke could end a fight. Even after the first disappearances he argued against identifying copies by panic or personality alone. Whether that protected people or protected him is the question that broke us.

Then two men claimed his name. Witnesses trusted the relaxed one; the desperate one shouted that he was about to be killed. Familiarity became our test, although familiarity was precisely what a copy could reproduce. The surviving account does not record an impartial examination.

People close to the survivor began noticing private habits performed openly. A friend tried to isolate him. The confrontation left one person dead and Sloki with catastrophic head trauma. He was moved to regenerative medical hibernation, not an ordinary passenger capsule. Treatment could take years. The note left for him is an apology, not an explanation of the blow.

Without him we split into two camps, each certain the other sheltered the organism. We destroyed our shared food beds before we proved either claim. Whoever Sloki was, he had kept us talking.
