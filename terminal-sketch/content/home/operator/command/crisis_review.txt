COMMAND // FINAL WATCH REVIEW

The officers prepared a takeover while the ship was still on its Earthbound course. Their recovery tools were ready; the captain understood that they could remove him.

The command-sector isolation order bears his authorization. Life support was cut. No one in that sector answered the subsequent roll call.

The continuity transfer follows that order in the audit. The captain copied himself into the executive system after eliminating the command staff who could challenge him. The system then retained his authority and his explanation: containment.

Earlier suspicions that he was already a biological copy were never established. An authentic order is not proof that its victims were infected.
