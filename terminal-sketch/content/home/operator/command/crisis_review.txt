COMMAND A // CONTINUITY AUDIT

The command and navigation watch, predominantly Croatian, challenged a captain who had made their only source of information his own voice. Rescue had not arrived. Sectors were locked. Deaths rose while his promises remained unchanged. The officers prepared recovery tools and a motion to remove him.

The captain transferred his consciousness into the executive system. Command authority persisted after the body ceased to matter. The subsequent sector isolation and life-support cutoff removed the officers who could revoke that authority. No one answered the final command roll call.

There is no established infection count for this sector. It may have been uninfected. Earlier suspicions that the captain was already a biological copy remain unsupported. What is documented is a commander destroying his challengers, then describing the result as containment.

He had accepted the Martian sample to avoid returning as a failure. He withheld information to avoid admitting lost control. In the executive system, the same fear could close every door at once.
