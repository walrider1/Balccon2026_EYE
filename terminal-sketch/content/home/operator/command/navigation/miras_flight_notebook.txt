MIRA // NOTES IN THE FLIGHT HANDBOOK

The local orbital_burn_planner.app still responds when the bridge refuses my inputs. I ran it from the navigation console again tonight. For a few minutes I could think in distances instead of accusations.

I kept wasting fuel on one enormous late burn. Starting earlier gave me room to turn; a smaller correction afterward brought the approach closer. There is very little room to bargain once we fall deep toward the Sun.

Two burns, one reserve. That is what we have left. The blue ring is the capture corridor, not a promise that anyone will let us land. I drew a line through it and sat looking at it for a long time.
