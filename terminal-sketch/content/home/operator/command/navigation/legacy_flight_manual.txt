NEREID FLIGHT HANDBOOK // MANUAL TRANSFER PROCEDURE

When executive course control is unavailable, Navigation officers use the local orbital burn planner.
Launch installed planners from their directory with the RUN command.

An emergency departure burn should be made before the vessel falls too deeply toward the primary. A later correction burn is standard practice; it costs less than trying to force one perfect maneuver.

Flight policy limits a manual transfer plan to two nodes and the reserve delta-v shown by the planner. Earth capture is confirmed only when the predicted line enters the blue capture ring.
