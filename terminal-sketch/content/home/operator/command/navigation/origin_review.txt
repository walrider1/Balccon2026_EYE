RECOVERED COMPARISON // PATIENT ZERO

The completed chronology places the first two Kovac subjects after the onboard sample contact, before the wider sector cases. The body eliminated during the first identification dispute matches the pre-contact chain of custody. The calm survivor carries the discontinuities later found in replicated tissue. That survivor is the patient placed in regeneration chamber 07.

The original panicked. The copy resembled the man everyone preferred to remember. They chose familiarity, and the first successful copy continued under Samuel's name. It reassured the laboratory while replication spread. Later it also prevented lynchings, remembered affection and argued for peace. Those acts do not undo its origin; its origin does not explain away every choice.

Your head injury, prolonged treatment and interrupted memory leave you with an inheritance you did not knowingly choose. A copied memory is not an order. Whatever made you, the next decision is yours. The sealed passengers still cannot be classified with certainty.
