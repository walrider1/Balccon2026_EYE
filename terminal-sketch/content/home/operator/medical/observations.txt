MEDICAL D // DIFFERENTIAL IDENTITY STUDY

This was meant to be a place of care. Patients arrived from every isolated sector; several later appeared on two incompatible ward registers. Our predominantly Slovenian medical team began comparing blood panels, scars and private memories. None reliably separated an original from a duplicate. Bringing every suspected case here may have helped the phenomenon spread.

The subjects retain appearance, professional skills and intimate recollections. Some reproduce grief too neatly, laugh at an inappropriate moment or repeat a private habit in public. These are observations, NOT A DIAGNOSTIC TEST. Fear, sleeplessness, injury and substances produce the same signs in ordinary people. One frightened patient was condemned for failing a question whose answer the examiner remembered incorrectly.

Contact appears to precede some cases, with a delay rather than an immediate change. A Martian sample is a working hypothesis, not an established source. Incomplete contact logs and Command's ban on direct sector communication prevent reconstruction. We cannot establish who was first. Do not mistake missing information for a negative result.
