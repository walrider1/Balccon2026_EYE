MEDICAL // DIFFERENTIAL IDENTITY REVIEW

Observed duplication can preserve appearance, remembered details and learned behaviour. Matching a memory or a biological sample is therefore not sufficient to establish which individual is the original.

Behavioural accusations are also inconclusive. Trauma, exhaustion, fear and substances can all alter a person's response. Calmness is not proof of humanity; panic is not proof of replacement.

Patient Kovac is undergoing treatment for severe head trauma. This record is not a diagnosis of his origin. Treatment and patient-safety access must not depend on an unverified accusation.
