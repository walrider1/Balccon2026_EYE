Brother, I left you a little help. Plants were always more your thing than passwords.
I keep replaying those last seconds. You should never have woken up like this.
It is too late for me. It is not too late to bring the ship home.

I want you to bring us home. I know what I am asking. I have stopped pretending I know who is still aboard.

The people who treated you kept a record of where you slept and another of what they did to wake you. Those who listened to the outside world kept their own account when the bridge began changing its story. I used to resent the paperwork. Now it may be the only thing he could not rewrite.

The patient-safety controller still answers to the person in the chamber. Remember that if his voice tells you to go back to sleep.
You always knew which way home was. I believe you will do the right thing.
